Country Content Design Backlog
Centralized follow-up list for country content simplified during roster design
This document is the single source for country-specific content work created when separatist movements, autonomous administrations, civil-war factions, or related actors are consolidated into a parent-country design.
Regional rosters, the global registry, and the current non-archived design bible should retain only starting representation decisions and a short reference to this backlog. Detailed country-content requirements, event-chain reminders, mechanic follow-up, and unresolved country-content work belong only here.
Backlog Rules
* Every entry remains open until its dedicated country-content review is completed.
* Moving content here does not change any starting country, ownership, subject, tag, playability, or classification decision.
* New simplifications identified later should be added here and removed from detailed notes in other project documents.
* Archived documents are read-only and must not be updated or synchronized.
* The authoritative writable design bible is `EU5 Modern World Mod — Design Bible`; files whose titles begin `ARCHIVED —` are not work targets.
* Comparable unresolved separatist administrations consolidated into their recognized parent states should be added under that parent country here.
