# North America 1993 Country List

## Purpose

This file defines the initial Version 0.1 country setup for North America in the 1993 start date. It follows the Europe + Former Soviet Union country-list template and captures sovereign states, dependencies, indigenous/autonomy issues, separatist movements, and strategic territories.

North America is not the primary Version 0.1 focus region, but it must work as a convincing global-placeholder region because the United States is the central post-Cold-War superpower and Canada, Mexico, the North Atlantic, and the Arctic are all tied into NATO, NAFTA, migration, energy, trade, and sovereignty mechanics.

It should track:

- Country name or represented entity
- Proposed EU5 tag
- Capital or administrative center
- Government type
- Alignment / bloc
- 1993 status
- Implementation priority
- Notes for borders, conflicts, claims, autonomy, and future event chains

---

## Tag Rules

- Prefer recognizable three-letter tags.
- Avoid conflicts with existing EU-style tags where possible.
- Use placeholder tags until EU5 tag constraints are known.
- Use standard EU5 country and subject types wherever possible; do not invent custom entity or subject types unless the project owner explicitly approves them.
- Indigenous sovereignty, separatist movements, and internal autonomy disputes should be preserved for later mechanics even when not full tags.
- Rebel, crisis, autonomy, and event-spawn tags should be marked clearly so they are not confused with independent countries in the 1993 start.

---

## Implementation Notes

- USA, CAN, and MEX should be the only mandatory playable continental tags for the initial North America setup.
- QUE and ZAP may use special/autonomy/event treatment; Puerto Rico and the reviewed island dependencies are represented directly within their administering states, while Nunavut remains internal Canadian content.
- Avoid over-fragmenting the region in Version 0.1. The goal is a stable global placeholder with strong event hooks, not a complete North American internal-politics simulator.
- Puerto Rico and all reviewed Caribbean dependencies must remain synchronized as direct territories of their administering states, with no separate starting actors or technical tags.
- Greenland is a standard EU5 Vassal of Denmark. Its authoritative implementation is in the Nordic/Denmark setup; North America keeps only geographic and Arctic cross-references.
